# Jobs to Be Done

## Functional JTBD

### Capture
**When** I encounter something that might matter to us later, **I want** to put it somewhere in seconds, **so that** I do not need to organize it now to benefit later.

### Resurface
**When** an old shared idea becomes relevant, **I want** it brought back with context, **so that** we do not need to search again.

### Decide
**When** we want to do something but do not want endless browsing, **I want** a few choices based on what both of us like and our current constraints, **so that** deciding does not consume the experience.

### React
**When** my partner shares something, **I want** to express a signal with almost no effort, **so that** our shared context becomes clearer naturally.

### Ask without starting a chat
**When** there is something I want to know from my partner—playful, practical, emotional or intimate—**I want** to place a lightweight question in our shared space, **so that** I can initiate the interaction without turning it into a formal conversation or another chat thread.

### Express desire
**When** I want my partner to understand something I enjoy, want, fantasize about, am curious about or do not want, **I want** a transparent and low-friction way to express it, **so that** intimacy is easier to communicate rather than left to guesswork.

### Understand my partner's voluntarily shared preferences
**When** I want to create a romantic or intimate moment, **I want** relevant context my partner has intentionally shared to be easy to recall, **so that** I can be attentive without the system pretending to know what they currently consent to.

### Decline without drama
**When** I do not want to answer or participate in a prompt right now, **I want** Pass, Not now or non-response to be normal states, **so that** transparency does not become obligation.

### Remember preferences
**When** I need an idea for my partner later, **I want** to rediscover things they genuinely cared about, **so that** I can act thoughtfully without maintaining a formal list.

### Coordinate
**When** we share a practical goal/list, **I want** both of us to see the same current state, **so that** we avoid duplicated effort and fragmented updates.

### Budget an experience
**When** we know roughly what we want to spend, **I want** options filtered by that constraint, **so that** money supports decisions rather than becoming an accounting exercise.

## Emotional JTBD

### Feel remembered
I want evidence that small things I say matter and are not immediately forgotten.

### Feel known
I want our shared space to reflect our actual tastes, preferences and evolving relationship rather than generic couple content.

### Feel desired and able to express desire
I want affectionate, flirtatious and sexual expression to have a natural place in our shared digital home when we choose to use it.

### Feel safe saying “not now”
I want to know that openness does not create an obligation to answer or participate every time.

### Feel presence
When we are apart, I want lightweight traces of my partner that feel warm/playful without requiring conversation.

### Reduce decision fatigue
I want confidence that a small set of choices is already relevant to us.

### Preserve spontaneity
I want to save “someday” ideas without immediately turning them into plans/tasks/commitments.

## Social / relationship JTBD

### Build shared language
Create natural couple behaviors around “save this for us”, “put that in our app”, and lightweight questions/signals unique to their shared space.

### Make candid communication easier
Create structured openings for subjects partners want to share but might otherwise postpone, including desire, fantasies, kinks, preferences and boundaries.

### Convert attention into care
Allow noticing a preference today to become thoughtful action later.

### Create shared history
Over time, transform saved possibilities into completed experiences and memories without automatically turning intimate life into performance analytics.

## Jobs the product should not take
BetweenUs should not attempt to:
- determine whether a relationship is healthy;
- score either partner's effort;
- calculate sexual compatibility or libido scores;
- mediate arguments automatically;
- replace difficult/direct conversations;
- infer current consent from past preferences;
- prove where someone was/what they did;
- pressure participation or affection;
- teach one partner how to overcome the other's refusal;
- optimize engagement at the expense of the relationship.
