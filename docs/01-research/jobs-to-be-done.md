# Jobs to Be Done

## Functional JTBD

### Capture
**When** I encounter something that might matter to us later, **I want** to put it somewhere in seconds, **so that** I do not need to decide or organize it now to benefit from it later.

### Resurface
**When** an old shared idea becomes relevant, **I want** it brought back with its original context, **so that** we do not have to remember where it came from or search again.

### Decide
**When** we want to do something but do not want to browse endlessly, **I want** a few choices based on what both of us actually like and our current constraints, **so that** deciding does not consume the experience.

### React
**When** my partner saves something, **I want** to express interest/disinterest with almost no effort, **so that** our shared taste becomes clearer naturally.

### Remember preferences
**When** I need an idea for my partner later, **I want** to rediscover things they genuinely cared about, **so that** I can act thoughtfully without maintaining a formal list.

### Coordinate
**When** we share a practical goal or list, **I want** both of us to see the same current state, **so that** we do not duplicate effort or rely on fragmented chat updates.

### Budget an experience
**When** we know roughly what we want to spend, **I want** our options filtered by that constraint, **so that** money supports the decision instead of becoming an argument or accounting exercise.

## Emotional JTBD

### Feel remembered
I want evidence that the small things I say matter and are not immediately forgotten.

### Feel known
I want our shared space to reflect *our* tastes rather than generic couple content.

### Feel presence
When we are apart, I want lightweight traces of my partner that feel warm or playful without requiring a conversation.

### Reduce decision fatigue
I want confidence that a small set of choices is already relevant to us.

### Preserve spontaneity
I want to save “someday” ideas without turning them immediately into plans, tasks or commitments.

## Social/relationship JTBD

### Build shared language
Create a natural couple behavior around “save this for us” or “put that in our app.”

### Convert attention into care
Allow noticing a preference today to become a thoughtful action months later.

### Create shared history
Over time, transform saved possibilities into completed experiences and memories.

## Jobs the product should *not* take

BetweenUs should not attempt to:

- determine whether a relationship is healthy;
- score either partner's effort;
- mediate arguments automatically;
- replace difficult conversations;
- prove where someone was or what they did;
- create pressure to perform affection;
- optimize engagement at the expense of the relationship.
