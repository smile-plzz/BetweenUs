# Vision

## Vision statement

**BetweenUs is the private digital home where two consenting adults build the context of their shared relationship.**

It remembers what they care about, makes preferences useful, creates lightweight feelings of presence, helps them express affection and desire, and turns “we should” into “we did.”

## The future we are designing for

A couple should not need to reconstruct relationship context every time they make a decision or try to communicate something meaningful.

When they ask “what should we watch?”, the answer begins with what *they* wanted to watch.

When they ask “where could we go tonight?”, the system understands saved places, mutual taste, distance, time and rough budget.

When one partner wants to understand what excites, interests or intrigues the other, the shared space can contain voluntarily expressed preferences, fantasies, kinks, questions and changing boundaries rather than forcing them to reconstruct those conversations from memory.

When a question feels awkward to start in chat, either partner can place a Question Card into their shared room. The other can answer, pass, choose Not now or leave it alone. Submitted answers become visible shared context.

When they are apart, the space contains small signs that the other person has been there.

When they return after several days, it feels like returning to a home—not opening an overdue inbox.

## Five product pillars

### 1. Shared Memory
Preserve meaningful fragments and make them useful later.

### 2. Presence & Expression
Let the space feel inhabited through lightweight gestures, traces and intentional interactions without rebuilding messaging.

### 3. Intimacy & Desire
Treat romantic and sexual expression as a normal, serious dimension of a consenting adult relationship. Help partners communicate preferences, curiosity, fantasies, kinks, affection and desire while keeping participation voluntary and avoiding performance/compatibility scoring.

### 4. Shared Decisions & Discovery
Use shared context to reduce choice overload and introduce a small number of explainable possibilities.

### 5. Shared Living
Gradually support goals, plans, budgets and practical coordination without turning the relationship into project management.

## Product promise

BetweenUs should progressively understand three layers:

### You
Signals contributed by one partner: saves, ratings, answers, reactions, preferences and behavior intentionally expressed in the shared space.

### Them
Equivalent signals from the other partner.

### Us
The useful relationship between those signals: overlaps, differences, recurring patterns, unresolved possibilities, completed experiences and shared goals.

The **Us layer** does not erase two individual people. It makes voluntarily shared context useful to them together.

## Long-term experience
BetweenUs may support:
- shared capture from social apps/web;
- movies, shows, music and books;
- restaurants, recipes and food;
- dates, games and nearby activities;
- products, wishes and gift signals;
- trips/places and Wayfare integration;
- lists and lightweight household coordination;
- shared goals and rough-purpose funds;
- consensual location/presence experiences;
- affection and playful gestures;
- Shared Question Cards;
- intimate questions, preferences, fantasies, kinks and intentionally shared content;
- voting/mutual reactions;
- contextual resurfacing;
- finite explainable discovery;
- shared memory after experiences.

This describes a destination, not an instruction to ship everything simultaneously.

## Intimacy principle

BetweenUs should make candid desire easier to communicate without making participation obligatory.

The shared-space model is transparent: if a partner intentionally submits an answer, preference or intimate object, it is shared. At the same time, Answer, Pass, Not now and non-response remain legitimate states. Historical preference never equals present consent.

Intimacy should not be isolated as a tacky hidden “spicy” mini-app. It belongs naturally alongside dates, movies, memories, affection, travel and shared living, with stronger discretion/security where the data is more sensitive.

## North-star behavior

The product becomes meaningful when a couple naturally develops the reflex:

> **“Put that in our app.”**

A second signal of the digital-home thesis is when they use BetweenUs to express or ask something specifically because the shared-space interaction feels easier or more fitting than starting another chat conversation.

## Anti-vision
BetweenUs should never become:
- another general-purpose messenger;
- a relationship/compatibility score;
- a sexual-performance or libido tracker;
- a streak-driven engagement machine;
- a chore dashboard with romantic branding;
- a mechanism for monitoring a partner;
- a ledger proving who contributed more;
- an endless recommendation feed;
- an AI character inserted into the relationship;
- a coercion/persuasion engine;
- a replacement for direct communication or current consent.

## Design mantra

> **Make shared life easier to remember, easier to feel, easier to express, and easier to act on.**
