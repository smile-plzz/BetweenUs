# Vision

## Vision statement

**BetweenUs is the private digital home where two people build the context of their shared life.**

It remembers what they care about, makes their preferences useful, creates lightweight feelings of presence, and helps turn “we should” into “we did.”

## The future we are designing for

A couple should not need to reconstruct their relationship context every time they make a small decision.

When they ask “what should we watch?”, the answer should begin with what *they* have wanted to watch—not a generic catalog.

When they ask “where could we go tonight?”, the system should understand saved places, mutual taste, distance, available time and a rough budget.

When a birthday approaches, a partner should be able to rediscover things the other genuinely loved months earlier without maintaining a formal gift spreadsheet.

When they are apart, the shared space should contain small signs that the other person has been there.

When they return after several days, it should feel like returning to a home—not opening an overdue inbox.

## Product promise

BetweenUs should progressively know three layers:

### You
Signals contributed by one partner: saves, ratings, reactions, preferences and behavior intentionally expressed in the shared space.

### Them
The equivalent signals from the other partner.

### Us
The useful intersection and relationship between those signals: overlaps, recurring patterns, unresolved possibilities, completed experiences and shared goals.

The product's unique value increasingly comes from the **Us layer**.

## Long-term experience

BetweenUs may eventually support:

- shared capture from social apps and the web;
- movies, shows, music and books;
- restaurants, recipes and food ideas;
- date ideas, games and nearby activities;
- products, wishes and gift signals;
- trips and places, potentially through Wayfare integration;
- shared lists and lightweight household coordination;
- shared goals and rough-purpose funds;
- consensual location/presence experiences;
- affection, playful gestures and intimate expression;
- voting and mutual reactions;
- contextual resurfacing;
- finite, explainable discovery;
- shared memory after an experience is completed.

This list describes a destination, **not V1 scope**.

## North-star behavior

The product becomes meaningful when a couple naturally develops the reflex:

> **“Put that in our app.”**

That phrase implies four things have been earned:

1. capture is easier than losing the item;
2. both people trust the shared space;
3. they believe the app will make the item useful later;
4. BetweenUs has become part of the couple's language.

## Anti-vision

BetweenUs should never become:

- another general-purpose messenger;
- a relationship score or compatibility meter;
- a streak-driven engagement machine;
- a chore-management dashboard with romantic branding;
- a mechanism for monitoring a partner;
- a ledger for proving who contributed more;
- an endless recommendation feed;
- an AI character inserted into the relationship;
- a replacement for direct communication between partners.

## Design mantra

> **Make shared life easier to remember, easier to feel, and easier to act on.**
