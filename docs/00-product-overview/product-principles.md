# Product Principles

These principles are constraints, not marketing statements. When two desirable features conflict, they should help the team choose.

## 1. Two people, one shared space

BetweenUs is designed around a pair. Do not casually generalize core UX to groups, families or social networks.

## 2. Capture faster than organization

If users must stop and classify everything, the system has failed. Accept first; enrich later. A share action should usually be enough.

## 3. Organize behind the scenes

Categories exist to help retrieval and decisions, not to make users maintain taxonomy.

## 4. Conversations can stay elsewhere

Do not rebuild messaging simply because two users need to communicate. Build durable shared context and lightweight expression instead.

## 5. The house is smart; there is no digital roommate

Use AI and recommendation systems as infrastructure. Avoid a named/gendered assistant or personality that inserts itself socially between partners.

## 6. Resurface, don't nag

Contextual resurfacing is valuable. Repeated reminders, streaks and guilt are not. Returning should create curiosity rather than obligation.

## 7. Finite choices beat infinite feeds

When helping the couple decide, prefer a small set of actionable options with reasons over endless content.

## 8. Explain why

Recommendations should expose useful reasoning: saved before, both liked similar things, nearby, fits time, fits rough budget. Avoid opaque “because AI says so.”

## 9. Presence is mutual, not surveillance

Features that reveal location, activity or current viewing require clear mutual expectations, controls and symmetry. Relationship status is not blanket consent.

## 10. Preserve individuality without creating silos

The space is shared, but signals remain attributable. Two people can disagree. The system should model overlap without pretending the pair has one identical taste.

## 11. Money enables experiences

Use budget and goals to make shared choices possible. Do not make the default experience about debt, blame, contribution scoring or partner policing.

## 12. Intimacy is legitimate, not a gimmick

Affectionate and sexual expression can belong in a private couple space. Design it with the same seriousness given to privacy, consent and accidental exposure.

## 13. No maintenance tax

Every recurring manual action must justify itself. Prefer learning from natural behavior—saving, reacting, choosing, completing—over forms and profile upkeep.

## 14. Memory should become more valuable with time

A saved object should gain context through reactions, decisions, visits, ratings and memories rather than becoming stale database clutter.

## 15. Build the wedge before the world

The vision is a digital home. V1 is not. Prove the capture → resurface → decide behavior before expanding into every shared-life category.
