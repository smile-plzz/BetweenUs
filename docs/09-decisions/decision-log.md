# Decision Log

This log captures decisions already made in product discovery so future work does not repeatedly reopen them without new evidence.

## D001 — BetweenUs is broader than Wayfare
**Decision:** Keep Wayfare separate. BetweenUs may eventually integrate/reuse its travel concepts.

**Reason:** Wayfare solves a focused place/trip problem; BetweenUs is a shared-life environment. Merging now would bloat both.

## D002 — Product metaphor is a digital home for two
**Decision:** Use “private digital home/shared room” as the conceptual model.

**Reason:** The product combines utility, memory, presence and intimacy; “organizer” is too narrow.

## D003 — Do not build another messenger
**Decision:** Messaging is not a core product goal.

**Reason:** Existing messengers solve conversation well. BetweenUs should preserve durable context and enable lightweight expression.

## D004 — Exactly two is a design primitive
**Decision:** Core product is for one paired couple, not groups.

**Reason:** Mutual preferences, shared context and presence are structurally different from group collaboration.

## D005 — Shared space, not private silos
**Decision:** Content intentionally added to BetweenUs is shared. No separate “mine” vault is part of the current concept.

**Reason:** The product represents an explicitly shared digital space.

**Caveat:** Feature-specific consent remains necessary for sensitive presence/location/intimate capabilities.

## D006 — Surprises happen outside the shared space
**Decision:** Do not add secret planning merely to support gifts/surprises.

**Reason:** A partner can use shared preference history as inspiration and plan privately elsewhere.

## D007 — Intelligence is not personified
**Decision:** No named/gendered AI butler/third personality.

**Reason:** It risks making the shared space feel socially crowded or weird. Intelligence should be environmental.

## D008 — North-star wedge
**Decision:** V1 centers on **“Never lose a ‘we should do this’ idea.”**

**Reason:** It is concrete, frequent and testable; the full digital-home vision is too broad to validate at once.

## D009 — Desired habit
**Decision:** Optimize for the spontaneous behavior **“Put that in our app.”**

**Reason:** This indicates capture is easy and future value is trusted.

## D010 — Discovery is finite
**Decision:** Recommendations should be a small explainable set, not an infinite feed.

**Reason:** The product exists partly to reduce decision fatigue.

## D011 — Return without guilt
**Decision:** After absence, show meaningful traces/changes rather than unread-count pressure or streak loss.

**Reason:** The shared-home metaphor should create curiosity, not obligation.

## D012 — Presence can be part of closeness
**Decision:** Explore co-presence and playful gestures.

**Reason:** The user explicitly values the feeling of inhabiting the digital space together.

**Constraint:** Presence must be consensual, understandable and not covert monitoring.

## D013 — Intimacy belongs in the vision
**Decision:** Romantic/sexual expression is a legitimate product domain for consenting adult couples.

**Reason:** A shared home that artificially excludes intimacy would not reflect the intended use.

**Constraint:** Treat it as sensitive content, not engagement bait.

## D014 — Money is decision support, not expense police
**Decision:** Explore rough budgets/shared purpose goals rather than contribution/debt policing.

**Reason:** Money should enable dates/trips/experiences without turning the product into relationship accounting.

## D015 — V1 before ecosystem
**Decision:** Validate capture → resurface → decide before building the broad feature map.

**Reason:** Two-person adoption and retention are the largest unresolved risks.
