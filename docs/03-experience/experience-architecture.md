# Experience Architecture

## Experience goal
BetweenUs should feel like **entering a shared place**, not opening a productivity dashboard or another messenger.

The room metaphor is emotional and architectural, not necessarily graphical. V1 should avoid expensive literalism such as 3D rooms unless later research demonstrates value.

The product supports the whole relationship with context-specific tone: practical, affectionate, playful, sexually explicit where mutually comfortable, and spiritually reverent where faith features are enabled. One domain must not sanitize another.

## Core loops

### Memory loop
**Capture → Remember → Understand → Resurface → Decide → Do Together → Learn**

### Expression loop
**Express → Respond voluntarily → Understand each other → Create shared context**

Both loops should converge on the same shared home and shared object model.

## Proposed top-level model

### Home / Our Space
The living surface. It shows a deliberately small set of meaningful current objects:
- partner presence/trace where appropriate;
- something left for you;
- a few recent shared captures;
- one timely resurfaced idea;
- an open Question Card where useful;
- a spiritual invitation/reflection where enabled;
- quick “What should we…?” entry points;
- shared goal/upcoming moment if relevant.

Home must not become a feed, inbox or dashboard.

### Our Things
The durable shared collection. Search/filter by type and state. Candidate views include Watch, Eat, Go, Do, Want, Listen/Read, Travel, Intimacy, Faith and Everything. These are views over one object model, not disconnected mini-products.

### Questions / Shared Prompts
Question Cards are “chatting without becoming chat.” Either partner or, where appropriate, the system can place a structured prompt in the shared space. The recipient can Answer, Pass, choose Not now, or leave it unanswered without penalty.

Question Cards can cover dates, food, movies, memories, affection, sexual desire/preferences/boundaries, faith/reflection and playful hypotheticals. Submitted answers are shared and attributable. Historical answers never equal current consent.

Detailed specification: `shared-question-cards.md`.

### Decide
Intent-first decision surface: **What should we do right now?**

Select intent + minimal constraints → receive 3–5 explainable candidates → react/vote → choose. Shared history should lead; external discovery fills gaps.

### Memories / Done
Things that became experiences. Utility gradually becomes relationship history. Sensitive sexual history and worship should not silently become performance analytics.

## Relationship dimensions

### Presence & affection
Lightweight co-presence, traces, taps/kisses/gestures, and “leave something for you.” The goal is closeness without reconstructing messaging.

### Intimacy & Desire
A first-class adult relationship dimension, not a hidden novelty mode. It can support candid desires, fantasies, kinks, preferences, boundaries, explicit Question Cards, and consensual playful/sexual expression.

Tone follows the couple and moment: romantic, playful, direct, explicit/raw, kinky or another mutually comfortable style. Faith features must not sanitize this language. Equally, sexual tone must not leak into unrelated contexts.

Participation remains voluntary. Answer / Pass / Not now / ignore are valid states. Preference, fantasy or prior participation is never evidence of present consent. Avoid libido, compatibility, sexual-performance or participation scoring.

### Spiritual Intimacy / Faith Together
For couples who enable it, support shared spiritual companionship. The initial researched model is Islamic: prayer-together invitations, duas, Qur'an/authenticated-hadith reflections, faith-oriented Question Cards and later seasonal/shared-practice experiences.

This is companionship, not religious monitoring: no piety score, partner prayer comparison, missed-prayer policing or AI judgment of religiosity. Religious source material requires provenance and should not be fabricated by generative AI.

Detailed specification: `spiritual-intimacy-islam.md`.

## Onboarding
1. **Promise** — “A private digital home for the two of you.”
2. **Create identity** — minimal profile.
3. **Create space** — no complex customization.
4. **Invite partner** — link/code/deep link.
5. **Partner explicitly accepts shared-space premise.**
6. **First joint action** — save/react to real things or answer one lightweight Question Card.
7. **Teach capture** — show how to share something from another app.

Avoid giant preference questionnaires. Sensitive domains such as explicit intimacy, presence and Faith Together should be understandable and configurable rather than silently assumed.

## Capture journey
Social/media app → Share → BetweenUs → optional short note → Save. The item appears immediately; enrichment can continue asynchronously.

**Save first, understand second.** Never reject a meaningful item because extraction failed.

## Partner reaction journey
Partner sees a new object in context and can react in one tap. No text reply is required. Their response improves later retrieval/resurfacing without turning reaction into homework.

## Question Card journey
1. Partner or system chooses/writes a prompt.
2. Card enters Our Space.
3. Other partner can Answer, Pass, Not now or ignore.
4. Submitted response becomes visible shared context.
5. Card resolves/fades naturally rather than becoming inbox debt.
6. Only deliberately useful answers should become durable memory/context.

For sexual questions, intensity of wording does not alter the participation model. For faith questions, non-response must not become religious guilt.

## Decision journey example: tired Thursday night
1. Open BetweenUs.
2. Tap **Watch something**.
3. Optionally choose “easy/funny” or available time.
4. System offers three choices: one saved by A, one saved by B, and eventually one external discovery.
5. Each explains why it fits.
6. Both react; overlap is clear.
7. Choose and leave the app.

The product wins when screen time ends quickly.

## Return journey after three days
Avoid “14 missed updates.” Prefer a finite **While you were away** state: something left for you, a few new ideas, a relevant open/answered card, an old idea that became timely, or an enabled spiritual reflection/invitation. Once seen, the user is caught up.

## Presence journey
Low-sensitivity examples: partner is currently in Our Space, partner reacted to the same object, playful gesture. Higher-sensitivity examples: current viewing, live location, intimate activity/content context. Higher-sensitivity presence requires explicit controls and must not be inferred from pairing.

## “Leave something for you”
A primitive for emotional presence: photo, song, short note, saved item, affectionate/playful gesture, intentional intimate object, or spiritual reflection. It should feel like leaving something in the room, not sending another chat message.

## Information hierarchy
Every shared item should answer:
1. **What is it?**
2. **Why is it here?**
3. **What do we think?**
4. **Can we act on it?**
5. **What happened?**
6. **Is it sensitive?** presentation/notification/retention rules where relevant.

## Notification philosophy
Notify only when immediacy has value: pairing, intentional “for you” object, explicit synchronous interaction, time-sensitive plan/invitation. Prefer in-app resurfacing for ordinary captures/recommendations.

Sensitive sexual and faith content should support generic notification wording and controllable lock-screen previews. Never expose explicit content unexpectedly.

## Accessibility and discretion
- discreet notification mode;
- controllable lock-screen previews;
- no unexpected sensitive images in widgets/previews;
- navigation not dependent only on color/emojis;
- reduced-motion support;
- explicit visibility state before sharing sensitive answers/content.
