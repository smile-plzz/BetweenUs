# Open Questions

These are intentionally unresolved. Future discussion/research should update the decision log when an answer is strong enough.

## Positioning

1. Is “digital home for two” understandable enough externally, or mainly an internal product metaphor?
2. What initial audience has the strongest pain: dating couples, long-distance couples, cohabiting couples, newly married couples, or another segment?
3. Does the working name **BetweenUs** remain appropriate after market/trademark/domain research?

## First-use experience

4. What useful experience can occur before the collection has enough history?
5. What convinces Partner B to install after Partner A sends an invite?
6. Should the first shared action be capture, a playful interaction, or a small guided decision?

## Capture

7. Which external share sources matter most in the first real pilot?
8. Is one optional note enough to preserve “why I sent this” context?
9. How much automatic categorization can be wrong before users lose trust?
10. How should screenshots/non-link content be captured without creating heavy AI/privacy dependencies?

## Reactions

11. What is the smallest reaction vocabulary that captures useful preference differences without feeling like rating homework?
12. Should the contributor also rate immediately, or is saving itself an implicit positive signal?

## Home / room

13. How literal should the room metaphor become visually?
14. What should the default home contain once there are hundreds of objects?
15. What is the right balance between “what changed” and “what matters now”?

## Presence

16. Which presence signals genuinely create closeness?
17. Is “partner is viewing this right now” delightful or pressuring in external user tests?
18. Should playful gestures persist as traces or disappear after delivery?

## Intimacy

19. Should intimate content be a category, a presentation mode, or simply ordinary shared objects with sensitivity metadata?
20. What controls are needed to prevent accidental notification/widget exposure?
21. How should changing consent/preferences be represented without making the experience clinical?

## Decision support

22. Which first decision domain creates the strongest repeat value: movies, food/places, or activities?
23. What number of candidates feels helpful rather than restrictive?
24. When should external discovery appear relative to saved items?
25. How should the system handle strong disagreement?

## Finance

26. Is rough per-date budget sufficient, or do users want shared monthly experience budgets?
27. Can purpose funds work without connecting bank accounts?
28. What financial data is valuable enough to justify its privacy burden?

## Travel / Wayfare

29. Should BetweenUs hand off to Wayfare, embed a Wayfare view, or share a backend model later?
30. Which product owns saved places if both are used?

## Data lifecycle

31. What happens to shared content when a couple unpairs?
32. Can both independently export a copy?
33. How are deletion requests handled for content contributed by one but jointly enriched by both?
34. What safety mechanism immediately revokes sensitive presence/location access?

## Business model

35. Is this consumer subscription, one-time purchase, freemium, or intentionally free during validation?
36. Which features could ethically support monetization without exploiting relationship anxiety or selling sensitive data?

## Validation

37. What behavior threshold justifies moving beyond founder testing?
38. How many external couples are sufficient for the first qualitative validation round?
39. What result would cause us to stop or radically reposition BetweenUs?
